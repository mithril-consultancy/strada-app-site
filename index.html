I · The app

Built around the floor, not the admin.

Strada is the internal app for the Strada sales team. It ranks the morning queue, briefs every call, files what agents say out loud, and writes it all straight back to the company CRM.

Agents use it, because it was built around how the floor actually sells. This site is the app's home: what it does, and where to reach support.

II · For agents

The whole day, in one hand.

Queue, matching, briefs, follow-ups: the day runs from the phone and is written straight back to the CRM.

  • The morning queue is already ranked. Who to call first and the reason why, before anyone opens a spreadsheet.
  • Speak instead of type. A voice memo becomes the CRM update, and the changes are ticked off before it saves.
  • Matched against the whole book. Every lead is scored against every listing in the org, not only the ones you happen to own.

IV · The instruments

Six instruments. One machine.

All in the app, all running at Strada today.
IV.i · Guida AI

The co-pilot on every deal.

An AI agent trained on sales, with oversight of every client's needs across your whole book. Powered by Claude.

IV.ii · Reactivation

Dead leads, worked back to life.

Scores the leads you wrote off and hands the best of them back to your agents. At Strada, 1 in 4 closed buyers come from this queue.

IV.iii · Matchmaker

Every listing finds its buyer.

Works through your listings and recommends the leads to contact, across the whole sales org, inside Guida.

IV.iv · The voice memo

Say it once. It files itself.

Speak a note when you come out of a call or a viewing. It finds the lead, writes the summary, updates the fields and books the next action. You tick what is right before anything saves.

IV.v · Owners

Where the next listing comes from.

Every other instrument here works demand. This one works supply. We bring the owner base, nearly half a million units, and turn it into a ranked call list for one tower.

  • Reads and writes your actual Salesforce. The answer is the record, not a summary of it.
  • Every recommendation carries its evidence: the signals that scored it, and what it still doesn't know.
  • Flags the question it can't answer yet, instead of guessing. "Decision timeline unclear, ask on the next call."
  • The queue is recomputed every night at midnight, Dubai time. It is never yesterday's list.
  • Every call is transcribed onto the lead's timeline, and dormancy is judged from that, not a last-touched date nobody updated.
  • Routed by specialty, so a rentals agent never gets handed a buyer's lead.
  • Matches against the whole org's inventory, not only the listings you happen to own.
  • Flags a listing whose views are running below comparables, before the owner asks why.
  • Broker blasts in the deal groups parsed into structured listings, screenshots included. The same unit across ten groups collapses into one.
  • It finds the lead out of your own pipeline, from the name and whatever detail you happened to mention.
  • The commitments come out with it: the budget, the area, the viewing, the date and time promised, and a follow-up message ready to send.
  • Every change is proposed, not applied. What the agent ticks is written to Salesforce in Salesforce's own format, so the reporting still adds up.
  • Ranked by when the tenancy ends. An owner is worth calling in the notice window, and the list knows the date.
  • The brief opens with what the owner paid, what the same stack sold for, and every call the firm has already made.
  • Do-not-contact is enforced on the server, and every dial files itself to Salesforce. Dials made offline replay on reconnect.
  • Sort the floor by leads at risk, not just by who is winning. A quiet book shows up before the month does.
  • Every agent against their number, billed rather than forecast, with the activity that explains the gap.
